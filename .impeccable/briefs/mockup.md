# Surface brief: mockup (main investigation screen)

Mode: Operate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The emissions gap read like a geological survey sheet: flat tinted units, hatching for uncertainty, a legend you trust. Refuses the dark neon "AI climate dashboard" with glowing globe and KPI cards.
OWN-WORLD: Light survey-paper ground, ink-green title block as shell, flat geologic tints (ochre → carmine ramp for the gap, sea-grey ocean), sector colours borrowed from the geologic time scale (Carboniferous teal for coal power, Permian red for steel…), hatching = low confidence. Barlow / Barlow Condensed labels, tabular figures.
STORY: A journalist spins the globe, sees where declared ≠ observed, opens a country, finds the sites behind the gap, asks the analyst, gets a sourced report.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink title bar (brand, tabs, synthetic-data flag). Left rail: metric + sector legend/filters. Centre: orthographic globe with map-legend box and scale. Right: country sheet (declared vs observed gap band, € value, confidence, top sites table, Generate report). Analyst chat docked bottom-right of centre, driving the map.
FORM: Geological survey map, candidate 7 of 7. seed:442b237299a32b7b
SIGNATURE: the declared/observed gap drawn as a hatched stratum between two layers, everywhere a gap appears.
RAISES: from zoo-gardens map, one geometry (country paths) drives map, selection and clipping; from Ikeda, numeric density in tabular columns.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
